# Protocol: one owner network, worked all the way through

Two people run this: the reader (the founder, in a browser, on the
live site) and the analyst (the working session, which cannot reach
the site and analyzes what the reader hands over). That split is not
a workaround; it is the point. The site is built for a person with a
browser and a question, so the person with the browser is the
instrument, and the analyst is kept honest by seeing only what the
site actually gave.

## 1. The question comes first

Write down the question before touching the site, verbatim, in the
words you would say to another person. The exercise measures the site
against the question, never the question against the site, and a
question adjusted mid-walk to fit what the pages offer would measure
nothing. A genuinely held question beats a constructed one; the site
exists because of one. If no live question is at hand, the fallback
pick is by rule rather than judgment, so the exercise never singles
anyone out: the alphabetically first owner name disclosed at twenty
or more facilities, in three or more states, carrying two or more
distinct roles in the current batch.

## 2. The walk

Drive the site as yourself, not as its maintainer. At every screen,
record five things, in the moment rather than reconstructed later:

- what you asked of this screen, in plain words;
- what the screen answered;
- what you needed to already know for the answer to make sense;
- where you left the site to answer something, and where you went;
- what you copied down by hand, because copying by hand is the
  clearest signal a surface is missing.

The expected stations are the search you arrive by, the owner's
explorer view, the pre-rendered owner page, and the facility pages
behind the footprint, but the walk goes wherever the question leads,
and a station the walk never needed is itself a finding.

## 3. The handoffs

At every surface with a CSV button, download the CSV. Those files
are the data legs the analyst can work directly, since they are the
site's own exports of the published record. Screenshots wherever a
screen confused or surprised; the confusion is data.

## 4. The report

Iterated between reader and analyst, outside this repository. The
report answers the original question strictly from what the site
gave, and marks every point where the answer needed something more,
split into the two kinds that must never be confused: things the
site refuses by charter, which should read in the report as the site
working, and things the site merely lacks, which are the findings.
A refusal misread as a gap would push the site toward the things it
exists to not do.

## 5. What feeds back

Gaps become candidate work items through the normal machinery:
founder rulings on anything that touches the charter, red-first
gates on anything that ships, dated entries for anything decided.
Nothing moves from the report to the site as a shortcut, and the
report itself never becomes site copy.
